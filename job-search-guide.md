## 5. Strategy to Overcome "No Degree" Barrier

### What works at $200K+ without a degree:

1. **GitHub Portfolio** — Your #1 credential. Showcase:
   - ML projects (model training, fine-tuning, deployment)
   - Full-stack applications with ML integration
   - Open-source contributions to ML frameworks

2. **Certifications (fast & cheap alternatives to degrees):**
   - **Google Machine Learning Engineer Certificate**
   - **AWS Machine Learning Specialty Certification**
   - **Microsoft Azure AI Engineer Associate**
   - **DeepLearning.AI / Coursera ML Specialization** (Andrew Ng)
   - These carry real weight with hiring managers

3. **Kaggle Competitions** — Ranked Kaggle profile demonstrates real ML skill

4. **Blog / Technical Writing** — Write about your ML projects on Medium or a personal site

5. **Target "or equivalent experience" listings** — Most $200K+ ML roles now say "BS in CS *or equivalent practical experience*"

6. **Network on LinkedIn** — Connect with ML hiring managers. Many teams care about what you can build, not where you studied.
